About Gagan BP – Freelance Technical Partner, India

Helping global clients with automation, data analytics, and web development.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Gagan BP, a freelance technical partner based in Bangalore, India. My journey into this field began during my MCA, where I worked on an academic project analyzing suicide trends in India. That experience shaped how I view technology and data—not just as numbers or tools, but as a way to understand real-world problems and support better decisions.

Today, I work at the intersection of analysis, development, and automation. My goal is simple: to help individuals and small teams make better use of their data and systems without unnecessary complexity.

Here's how I typically help:

🤖 Automation I design custom n8n workflow automation systems, MCP agents, chatbots, and lead generation pipelines. This includes AI-assisted automations that reduce repetitive manual work—so tools work together more efficiently and teams can focus on higher-value tasks.

📊 Data Analytics I work with Power BI, Python, SQL, and Excel to organize raw or messy datasets and convert them into clear, meaningful dashboards and reports. The focus is always on clarity, accuracy, and usability.

🌐 Web Development I build clean, lightweight portfolio websites and landing pages using HTML, CSS, and modern frontend technologies. These are designed to be simple, fast, and practical, helping people present their work professionally.

I've developed this skill set with startups, students, and freelancers in mind. I understand the reality of limited budgets, evolving requirements, and tight timelines. Rather than offering oversized solutions, I aim to provide practical, cost-effective technical support that fits real needs.

I strongly believe that good outcomes start with listening. Before building or analyzing anything, I focus on understanding your goals, constraints, and expectations. I prefer working collaboratively—asking questions, validating assumptions, and making sure the work remains useful, not just technically impressive. I don't claim expertise beyond my scope; I focus on delivering what has been agreed, clearly and responsibly.

Outside of work, I enjoy exploring Bangalore's food scene and experimenting with new AI and automation tools to keep learning. If you're looking for a technical partner who values clarity, honesty, and steady progress, I'm always open to a conversation.

📍 Location & Availability

Based in Bengaluru, India – serving clients in the US, UK, EU, Australia, and APAC regions. Available for remote collaboration across time zones.
